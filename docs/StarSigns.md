# Star Signs: setting signs by hand

## Unique idols

To give a unique idol a specific star sign, add this line to her `params.json`:

```json
"starsign": "Capricorn"
```

Use one of the twelve sign names, in any capitalisation: Aries, Taurus, Gemini, Cancer, Leo, Virgo, Libra, Scorpio, Sagittarius, Capricorn, Aquarius or Pisces.
