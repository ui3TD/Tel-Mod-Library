[h1]REQUIRES: IM-HarmonyIntegration[/h1]
Download IM-HarmonyIntegration here: [url=https://github.com/ui3TD/IM-HarmonyIntegration]GitHub Link[/url]

Adds hotkeys for the main menu based on the home row of the keyboard.

A - Idols
S - Staff
D - Activities
F - Singles
G - Media
H - Special Events
J - Research
K - Policies

To change these keys, install [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3283314791]ModMenus[/url] and open the Hotkeys tab of its Mod Settings menu. Without it, the keys are as listed above.

[url=https://github.com/ui3TD/Tel-Mod-Library]source code[/url]
