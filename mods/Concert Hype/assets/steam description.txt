A new concert minigame: manage each song's lineup to build and hold the crowd's hype.

[h3]Details[/h3]
- Each song is performed by its single's senbatsu, in the single's triangle formation.
- Before each song you fill the places of idols who can't perform, and you can swap in one idol from the bench. A changed lineup surprises the crowd: it pays off most while the crowd is below 100% hype.
- Idols tire as they perform and recover on the bench. A crowd at 100% hype or more carries them instead. Tired idols perform worse and are more likely to have an accident, which keeps them off stage for a few songs.
- Call an MC break between songs (twice per show) to pull the crowd's hype toward 100%.
- The show is scored on its average, its best moment and its finale. The score becomes the concert's hype in your records and for awards.
- Concert revenue no longer depends on hype: it pays what 200% hype paid before, so it depends only on ticket sales.
- Action cards, the accident wheel and MC breaks in the setlist are off. Other mods' changes to song hype don't apply during the show.
- Tick off the setting in the mod menu to play concerts as in the original game.
