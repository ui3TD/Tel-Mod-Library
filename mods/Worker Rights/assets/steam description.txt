Idols will demand fair wages! Brand new staff cannot be blamed for scandals!

[h3]Idol Salaries[/h3]
- 20000 yen/wk is the expected starting salary for 100% satisfaction.
- 20000 yen/wk is the default starting salary.
- In Unfair, idols at 10 fame will expect at least 10% of their average earnings as salary so that the endgame is more challenging. This never lowers what they would expect otherwise.

[h3]Graduation Dates[/h3]
The base game works out each week how an idol's graduation date should move, but never saves it. Now it does, with retuned numbers so busy idols don't stay forever.
- Later: salary satisfaction over 75% / 100% / 150% (+0.5 / +1 / +1.5 days a week), friendship with you of almost level 4 or more (+1), each flirt point (+1, up to 4), on hiatus (+2), contract work in the last 60 days (+0.5, plus 0.25 per week of work, up to +2; a one-off counts as one week, a longer contract counts every week it runs), shows (+1 / +1.5 / +2 for 1 / 2 / 3), latest single (+3.5 center, +1.5 2nd-3rd row, +0.5 further back), pushed (+1.5).
- Sooner: physical stamina under 25 (-1), mental stamina under 25 / 50 (-3 / -1), friendship with you of almost level -2 or less (-2), dating banned (-2), injured (-2), depressed (-7), no contract for over 60 / 90 / 120 / 180 / 360 days (-1 / -2 / -3 / -4 / -6). This counts from the day her last contract ended.
- In Unfair and Normal mode with manual salaries, underpaid idols graduate sooner: salary satisfaction under 50% moves the date 10 days closer each week, under 20% 30 days.
- The week's total is at most +5 days, so even the best-treated idols graduate around 30.
- Age: over 20 / 25 / 30 moves the date 1 / 3 / 7 days closer each week.

[h3]Staff Rights[/h3]
- Staff cannot be fired using scandal points within the first month so that you can't just hire and immediately fire staff to get rid of points.
