Download IM-HarmonyIntegration here: [url=https://github.com/ui3TD/IM-HarmonyIntegration]GitHub Link[/url]

Fans react on the Social Media tab to what happens at your agency, in posts and replies written by an AI service you choose. Fans start posting once your agency has 100 fans, and only the kinds of fans you have post. Each kind of fan is as happy or critical as their opinion of your group. The feed keeps 100 posts instead of 20.

[h3]What fans react to[/h3]
[list]
[*]Singles, concerts, tours, elections and cancelled elections
[*]The yearly awards show
[*]New shows, shows ending after a year or more, and ad or TV drama contracts
[*]New members, hiatuses, graduation announcements and graduations
[*]Public events and dating scandals
[*]Policy changes and firings that clear scandal points
[/list]

[h3]Refresh[/h3]
The Refresh feed bar at the top of the Social Media tab shows the next 5 waiting posts at once, and says how many are waiting. When none are, it says AI request: a press asks your AI service for everyday chatter about your group, shown as soon as it arrives. Refreshing doesn't count toward requests per week, and works even at 0.

[h3]Setting it up[/h3]
You need the [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3283314791]ModMenus mod[/url] and an account with an AI service. In Settings, open Mod Settings and fill in:
[list]
[*]AI service: OpenAI (default), Anthropic, or OpenAI-compatible for OpenRouter, Google, Ollama, LM Studio and others.
[*]Base URL: shown for OpenAI-compatible services; the service's address.
[*]Model: blank uses gpt-6-luna on OpenAI and claude-haiku-5-5 on Anthropic.
[*]API key: your key from the service.
[*]Requests per week: the most per in-game week.
[/list]
Then tick Turn on AI fan posts and press Apply. A notification tells you whether it worked. Later problems, such as a rejected key or a busy service, show at the top of the Social Media tab.

[h3]Which model to use[/h3]
Each request is charged to your AI service account. Tested on 21 October 2026, cost per in-game year at 2 requests a week:
[list]
[*]Cheapest: gpt-6-luna (OpenAI, default), 3 cents.
[*]Best value: xiaomi/mimo-v2.6-pro:floor (OpenRouter), 15 cents.
[*]Mid-priced, close to the facts: gpt-6-sol (OpenAI), 70 cents.
[*]Mid-priced, lively: gemini-4-argon (Google), 85 cents.
[*]Nearly the best: moonshotai/kimi-k3:floor (OpenRouter), 1.50 dollars.
[*]Best posts: claude-opus-5-5 (Anthropic), 2.40 dollars.
[/list]
The mod sends at most 20 requests an hour on its own, even when fast-forwarding. Each refresh with no posts waiting sends one more. To be safe, set a spending limit on your key.

[h3]Privacy[/h3]
Requests send the moment's details to your AI service: group and idol names, results, idol details such as age, popularity and trivia, and recent feed posts. Your API key stays in a file on your PC, never in your saves. To remove it, clear the key field and press Apply.

Your mod can ask for fan posts about its own events. See the [url=https://github.com/ui3TD/Tel-Mod-Library/blob/main/docs/AIFanChatterForModders.md]modders' guide[/url].

[h3]Removing the mod[/h3]
Your saves load normally without it. The feed goes back to 20 posts.
