ModMenus provides a UI menu to manage the settings of other mods.

Try one of these mods to see it in action:

[/list]

[h2]For Modders[/h2]
ModMenus reads 'JSON/Mod Menu/modmenu.json' in your mod to build its settings menu. Supported elements: text, sliders, checkboxes, dropdowns and text fields.

Format, examples and tips: [url=https://github.com/ui3TD/Tel-Mod-Library/blob/main/docs/ModMenus.md]ModMenus modding guide[/url]

[url=https://github.com/ui3TD/Tel-Mod-Library]source code[/url]

[h3]Changelog[/h3]
- 1.1.0: Mod Settings button now sits under Settings; sliders without a default start at the midpoint.
- 1.2.0: Fixed blank text rows, and sliders with raw-text labels, ranges ending at 0 or no default.
- 1.3.0: Settings start at their defaults; dropdowns open above other rows; bad menu files skipped.
- 2.0.0: New text field setting, so mods can let players type a name or other text.
- 2.1.0: Tidier menu: even spacing, aligned sliders, wider dropdowns, long text fits, blue scrollbar.
