[h1] IMPORTANT[/h1] 
This Steam Workshop mod only checks if IM-HarmonyIntegration (IM-HI) is installed on your system. The result is shown in the screenshot above.
If your IM-HI is out of date, the label says IM-HI out-of-date and a glowing yellow Update IM-HI button appears under Mods. Click it to open the IM-HI download page.

You must install the IM-HI plugin manually. Follow the instructions at this link:
- [b][url=https://github.com/ui3TD/IM-HarmonyIntegration]IM-HarmonyIntegration GitHub page[/url][/b]
