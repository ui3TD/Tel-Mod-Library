Rebalances the theater. Theater shows go stale over time. Release a single every month to keep the theater shows fresh.

[h3]Details[/h3]
- Theater show sales and subscriptions start to decay after 30 days since the last single due to staleness, levelling off at 10% of normal. It's not so severe in easy/normal mode, but more severe in Unfair.
- Theater subscription revenue decreased by 70% in Normal mode and 95% in Unfair mode. Most of the revenue goes to the streaming service provider.
- Theater attendance for Manzais is 30% of a performance's for every audience, so they earn about the same per stamina.
- Every theater target pays once you build that audience. Attendance per targeted fan depends on how hard singles and shows make that audience to build: hardcore and casual 1, a gender 1.05, adult 1.1, teen and young adult 1.3. 'Everyone' draws 0.5 per fan, like an unbuilt fanbase.
