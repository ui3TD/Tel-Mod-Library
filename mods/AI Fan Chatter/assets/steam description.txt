Download IM-HarmonyIntegration here: [url=https://github.com/ui3TD/IM-HarmonyIntegration]GitHub Link[/url]

Fans react on the Social Media tab to your singles, concerts, graduations and events, in posts and replies written by an AI service you choose. Each kind of fan is as happy or critical as their opinion of your group. The feed keeps 100 posts instead of 20.

[h3]Setting it up[/h3]
You need the [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3283314791]ModMenus mod[/url] and an account with an AI service. In Settings, open Mod Settings and fill in:
[list]
[*]AI service: OpenAI (default), Anthropic, or OpenAI-compatible for OpenRouter, Ollama, LM Studio and others.
[*]Base URL: only for OpenAI-compatible services.
[*]Model: blank uses gpt-6-luna on OpenAI and claude-haiku-5-5 on Anthropic.
[*]API key: your key from the service.
[*]Requests per week: the most per in-game week.
[/list]
Then tick Turn on AI fan posts and press Apply. A notification tells you whether it worked.

[h3]Which model to use[/h3]
Each request is charged to your AI service account. Tested on 21 October 2026, cost per in-game year at 2 requests a week:
[list]
[*]Cheapest: gpt-6-luna (OpenAI, default), 3 cents.
[*]Best value: xiaomi/mimo-v2.6-pro:floor (OpenRouter), 15 cents.
[*]Mid-priced, close to the facts: gpt-6-sol (OpenAI), 70 cents.
[*]Mid-priced, lively: google/gemini-4-argon (OpenRouter), 85 cents.
[*]Nearly the best: moonshotai/kimi-k3:floor (OpenRouter), 1.50 dollars.
[*]Best posts: claude-opus-5-5 (Anthropic), 2.40 dollars.
[/list]
The mod sends at most 20 requests an hour, even when fast-forwarding. To be safe, set a spending limit on your key.

[h3]Privacy[/h3]
Requests send the moment's details to your AI service: group and idol names, results, idol details such as age, popularity and trivia, and recent feed posts. Your API key stays in a file on your PC, never in your saves. To remove it, type - in the key field and press Apply.

[h3]Changing the AI's instructions (advanced)[/h3]
See the [url=https://github.com/ui3TD/Tel-Mod-Library/blob/main/docs/AIFanChatter.md]AI Fan Chatter prompt guide[/url].

[h3]Removing the mod[/h3]
Your saves load normally without it. The feed goes back to 20 posts.
