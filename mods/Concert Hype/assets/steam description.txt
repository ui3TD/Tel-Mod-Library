A new concert minigame: manage each song's lineup to build and hold the crowd's hype.

[h3]Details[/h3]
- Each song is performed by its single's senbatsu, in the single's triangle formation.
- Before each song you drag idols from the bench, which holds every idol in your agency who isn't on stage, onto the formation to swap them in, as many as you like, and drag two idols on stage onto each other to swap their places. Idols who can't perform are replaced by the best idol on the bench. A changed lineup surprises the crowd: it pays off most while the crowd is below 100% hype.
- While a song plays, each idol on stage spins a wheel like a single's marketing roll: its arcs are her chances of a critical failure, failure, success or critical success, and it lands on how she performed: a star for a critical success, a sparkle for a success, a nervous face for a failure and a sad face for a critical failure. On the formation, the bar under each idol's energy shows her chance of a success.
- Idols tire as they perform and recover on the bench. A crowd at 100% hype or more carries them instead. Tired idols perform worse and are more likely to have an accident, which keeps them off stage for a few songs.
- On the Talk Break tab, drag up to 3 idols onto the talk break's places to have them host a talk break before the next song (twice per show). It pulls the crowd's hype toward 100%, more with more hosts. The hosts don't rest during it.
- Songs and talk breaks play in 5 seconds instead of 10.
- The show is scored on its average, its best moment and its finale. The score becomes the concert's hype in your records and for awards.
- Concert revenue no longer depends on hype: it pays what 200% hype paid before, so it depends only on ticket sales.
- Action cards, the accident wheel and talk breaks in the setlist are off. Other mods' changes to the hype of songs and talk breaks don't apply during the show.
- Tick off the setting in the mod menu to play concerts as in the original game.
