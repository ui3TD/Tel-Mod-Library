Rebalances concerts to make things more challenging.

[h3]Details[/h3]
- Concert revenue formula rebalanced so it approaches zero with high ticket prices, and each venue becomes the best choice in turn as your fanbase grows.
- Concert venues can only be unlocked if you've sold out the previous venue with a profit.
- Club concerts keep the base game's hype bonus: 200% hype pays x2.0 at a club (x1.25 at other venues).
- In Unfair, Tokyo Coliseum is increased to 50,000 capacity, but has its base cost increased to 200,000,000 for more endgame challenge.
- In Unfair, the Open Air Stage base cost is reduced to 20,000,000 so it pays off between the concert hall and the stadium.
