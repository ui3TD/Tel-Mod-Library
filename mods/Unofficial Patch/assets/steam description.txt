[h3]No more frozen calendar[/h3]
When an error happened during the day change, the date stopped moving and the game couldn't continue. Now the game keeps running and shows a red notification naming what failed. When you report a bug, please include your Player.log.

[h3]Main changes[/h3]
These fixes change how the game plays. Each one makes the game work the way the developers most likely meant it to:
- Theater shows use stamina.
- World tour attendance depends on your fame, so big venues need high fame.
- The Best Employer award can be lost, and expected salaries follow recent pay.
- Senbatsu stats use the stat asked for, and don't punish rows you can't fill.
- Edgy and Artsy PVs change sales and new fans, as their tooltips say.
- Concerts, cancellations and events change your idols' fan opinion.
- Fans of one type (business deals, cafes, fans switching idols) land in that type and are never lost.
- A scandal can no longer raise your agency's fans by moving the same switching fans twice.
- Dating scandal responses cost mental stamina, except for Thick Skin and Shameless idols.
- Shows cast with the entire group give every idol the same opinion vote, not just the first idol.
- Song quality and the first single's own fan appeal now shape fan opinion votes.

Plus 34 smaller fixes to tooltips, text, saving and loading, and rare freezes: [url=https://github.com/ui3TD/Tel-Mod-Library/blob/main/docs/UnofficialPatch.md]full list of fixes[/url]
