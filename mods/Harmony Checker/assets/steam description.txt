[h1] IMPORTANT[/h1] 
This Steam Workshop mod only checks if IM-HarmonyIntegration (IM-HI) is installed on your system. The result is shown on the Mods button in the main menu:
- Mods [IM-HI not installed]: IM-HI is not installed.
- A green tick on the Mods button: IM-HI is installed and works.
- An orange ! on the Mods button: IM-HI is too old. A glowing yellow Update IM-HI button appears under Mods. Click it to open the IM-HI download page.
Hold the mouse over the tick or the ! to read what it means.

You must install the IM-HI plugin manually. Follow the instructions at this link:
- [b][url=https://github.com/ui3TD/IM-HarmonyIntegration]IM-HarmonyIntegration GitHub page[/url][/b]
